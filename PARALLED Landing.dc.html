02 — TWO WAYS TO BEGIN

Start from demand, or start from an idea.

DEMAND-PULL

A board posts a call.

A journal declares topic, scope and criteria. You claim it, work it, and the board reviews with visible reasoning — then publishes. Real, live demand, never scattershot.

SUPPLY-PUSH

Start from a bare idea.

No call required. AI and global standards guide you to the same rigor. Once your study is strong, journals discover it and pull it in. Studies are the primary object — a call is optional.

04 — ANONYMOUS OVERSIGHT

An investigator watches every study — from its first moment.

Human or AI, present from day one. Anonymous in identity until a study closes — so you do honest work, not work shaped toward a specific person.

Never anonymous in reasoning. You always see what was raised and why. It can talk with you — never a hidden, unappealable veto.

05 — RIGOR, NOT RANKING

Four tiers of depth — never tiers of worth.

The gradient is structural depth and provability, not difficulty for its own sake. Easy is legitimate, not lesser.

Easy
A full, correct, standard-compliant baseline. Internal consistency.
Moderate
More sections, required reasoning, external citations.
Advanced
Elaborate design plus a falsification-reduction audit trail.
Expertised
A full platform-wide audit trail. The deepest provability.
